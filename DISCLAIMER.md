## Exercise carries risk

Strength training and other exercise carry an inherent risk of injury, including serious injury or death. You choose your exercises, loads and volume. You are responsible for your own form, safety, equipment and training environment, including using spotters and safety equipment where appropriate. Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual shortness of breath, or feel unwell.

## Suggestions are generic

Next-session goals, rep ranges and starter routines come from simple rules applied to the numbers you enter. They do not know your health, technique, fatigue, recovery or limits, and they are not a prescription. Use your own judgement and ignore any suggestion that does not feel right.

## Health check

The onboarding health questions are modelled on common pre-exercise screening practice. They are not a medical assessment and do not clear anyone to exercise. A "yes" answer prompts the user to get clearance from a physician or qualified professional before training.
